I am **Leonard (Yuanjie Liu)**, an independent researcher. My main lines of work: **controlled fusion, agent research, and advancing mathematics**. I also study **force-sustaining models**. **Recent** - 🎙️ Podcast released - 📖 Published a book: *Judgment & Compounding* (《判断力与复利》) - 📱 Built a software package to put ten thousand agents into human phones — into your daily life - ⚛️ Working on the controlled-fusion Fusion project **Education** B.Eng. in Automation & Artificial Intelligence, Tianjin University of Commerce (2020–2024). **Experience** Research Assistant / Research Engineer, Zhejiang Binjiang Research Institute, AI & Systems (2025.08–2026.05); before that an independent research period (GAP, 2024.07–2025.08) building multi-agent collaboration prototypes, token incentive mechanisms, and decentralized compute scheduling. Three connected research lines: 1. **Controlled fusion** — HUSHFUSION Forge: an automated search / score / record / learn loop for electromagnetic coil design; fusion-sim: a digital fusion simulation workbench (TORAX / Gym-TORAX, gym-μ-FRC). 2. **Agent research** — DIAP decentralized agent identity protocol (zero-knowledge proofs + hybrid P2P), the bolloon agent resource network, and a phone-agent package that puts ten thousand agents into your phone. 3. **Advancing mathematics** — counting-based: physics reconstructed from flowing-space first principles (mass as displacement lines, ħ as angular momentum per radian, photons as excited electron helices), every theorem machine-checked in Lean 4 (Yang–Mills, HIBS, NODS — zero sorry, zero warning). **Other research** Force-sustaining models.
